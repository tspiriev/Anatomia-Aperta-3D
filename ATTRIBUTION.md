# Attribution

## Anatomia Aperta 3D

**Anatomia Aperta 3D** is an open-access human anatomy project built upon existing open anatomical resources and developed through extensive anatomical review, correction, reconstruction, expansion, organization, and 3D visualization.

The project would not have been possible without the work of the anatomists, researchers, 3D artists, and developers who created the original resources and made them openly available to the scientific and educational community.

Anatomia Aperta 3D gratefully acknowledges these projects and their contributors.

---

## BodyParts3D / Anatomography

A substantial part of the anatomical geometry underlying this project originates from **BodyParts3D/Anatomography**, developed by the Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), Japan.

**BodyParts3D** is a comprehensive three-dimensional anatomical database representing structures of the human body.

Official resources:

- BodyParts3D / Anatomography:

## Anatomia Aperta 3D Contributions

The source anatomical resources have been extensively reviewed and modified to the best of the author's anatomical knowledge.

The work performed for **Anatomia Aperta 3D** includes anatomical corrections, reconstruction of existing structures, creation of additional anatomical structures, reorganization of the datasets, modification of materials, and development of the models for interactive 3D visualization.

Particular emphasis has been placed on the **central, peripheral, and autonomic nervous systems**.

Major areas of development include:

- reconstruction and refinement of cerebral white-matter pathways;
- thalamic and hypothalamic anatomy;
- basal ganglia and basal forebrain structures;
- brainstem nuclei and pathways;
- cerebellar pathways;
- cranial nerves and their branches;
- spinal cord pathways;
- spinal nerve roots, dorsal root ganglia, and spinal nerves;
- cervical, brachial, and lumbar plexuses;
- sympathetic and parasympathetic nervous systems;
- vagal and visceral nervous pathways;
- autonomic ganglia and plexuses;
- cerebral and peripheral vascular anatomy;
- cerebral venous anatomy and dural venous sinuses;
- cranial and spinal meninges;
- musculoskeletal anatomy;
- cardiac anatomy and the cardiac conduction system.

In addition to anatomical modifications, the Anatomia Aperta 3D project includes development of the **anatomical hierarchy, model organization, materials, interactive 3D visualization, website, interface, and navigation system** through which the models are presented.

The complete chronological record of anatomical corrections and additions is available in:

**MODIFICATIONS.md**

This record currently documents **202 individual modifications and development steps**. Some anatomical structures appear more than once because they were progressively corrected or expanded during successive stages of development.

## Third-Party Components

Some components incorporated into the source datasets may originate from additional third-party anatomical resources.

Where such components are present, the original attribution and licensing requirements remain applicable.

Examples identified in the Z-Anatomy source documentation include anatomical material originating from:

- University of Dundee / CAHID;
- University of Dundee School of Medicine;
- Lissie Cowley;
- BodyParts3D / Anatomography;
- other resources acknowledged by the original Z-Anatomy project.

Third-party material is **not automatically relicensed** as original Anatomia Aperta 3D material.

Users should consult the original source documentation and the `LICENSE.md` file in this repository when reusing or redistributing individual components.

## Suggested Attribution

When using or redistributing Anatomia Aperta 3D models, we recommend the following attribution:

> **Anatomia Aperta 3D** — anatomical models derived from BodyParts3D/Anatomography (Database Center for Life Science) and Z-Anatomy, with anatomical corrections, reconstructions, additions, organization, and 3D visualization by Anatomia Aperta 3D.

Where appropriate, users should also cite and acknowledge the original BodyParts3D/Anatomography and Z-Anatomy projects.

If a model contains material originating from another third-party source, the attribution and licensing requirements of that source must also be preserved.

## Open Development

Anatomia Aperta 3D is intended to remain open and transparent.

Users are encouraged to consult the original datasets alongside the modified models provided here. Anatomists, clinicians, researchers, educators, and other users who identify anatomical inaccuracies or areas for improvement are encouraged to report them through the GitHub repository.

Corrections supported by anatomical literature and authoritative references are particularly welcome.
