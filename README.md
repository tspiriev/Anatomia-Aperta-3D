# Anatomia-Aperta-3D
Anatomia Aperta 3D — an open-access interactive human anatomy atlas based on BodyParts3D/Anatomography and Z-Anatomy, reorganized and expanded, with major additions to the nervous system
The project builds upon the open anatomical resources BodyParts3D/Anatomography and Z-Anatomy. These datasets were extensively reviewed, corrected, reorganized, and expanded with the aim of improving anatomical accuracy, completeness, and educational value.

Anatomia Aperta 3D would not have been possible without the work of the anatomists, researchers, 3D artists, and developers who created these resources and made them available to the scientific and educational community.
Links to BodyParts3D/Anatomography: https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/
Link to Z-Anatomy (official webpage and Github repository): https://www.z-anatomy.com/, https://sketchfab.com/Z-Anatomy, https://github.com/Z-Anatomy 

Purpose of the Project

The purpose of Anatomia Aperta 3D is not to replace the original datasets, but to build upon, refine, and extend them while preserving their open-access philosophy.

The source models were  corrected or reorganized where necessary, while numerous structures, pathways, nerves, plexuses, nuclei, and other anatomical components that were absent or insufficiently represented were reconstructed or added.

Particular emphasis has been placed on the nervous system, reflecting the author’s background in neurosurgery and neuroanatomy.

Major areas of work include:

* Central nervous system — reconstruction and refinement of white-matter association, commissural and projection pathways; thalamic and hypothalamic structures; basal ganglia and basal forebrain; brainstem nuclei and pathways; cerebellar circuitry; motor, sensory, visual, auditory, and limbic systems.
* Cranial and peripheral nerves — extensive correction and reconstruction of cranial nerves, orbital innervation, spinal nerves, cervical, brachial and lumbar plexuses, intercostal nerves, vagus and phrenic nerves, and other peripheral nerve branches.
* Spinal cord and spinal nerves — reconstruction of bilateral dorsal and ventral roots from C1 to S5, dorsal root ganglia, dorsal rami, cauda equina, spinal pathways, facet-joint innervation, and sinuvertebral nerves.
* Autonomic nervous system — extensive reconstruction of sympathetic and parasympathetic anatomy, including the sympathetic trunks and ganglia, rami communicantes, vagal pathways, cardiac and visceral nerves and plexuses, splanchnic nerves, periarterial plexuses, and hypogastric systems.
* Vascular and meningeal anatomy — additions and corrections to cerebral and facial vessels, cerebral venous anatomy, dural venous sinuses, meninges, meningeal vessels, and innervation.
* Musculoskeletal and cardiac anatomy — systematic refinement of muscular anatomy and the addition or improvement of cardiac structures, including the cardiac conduction system.

The development record currently documents 202 individual modifications and stages of anatomical reconstruction and can be found in a separate file (Modifications.md)
