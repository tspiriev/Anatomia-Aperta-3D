# Anatomia Aperta 3D — Modification Record

## Overview

This document provides the complete chronological record of the anatomical
corrections, reconstructions, additions, and visualization improvements
performed during the development of **Anatomia Aperta 3D**.

The atlas builds upon the anatomical resources provided by
**BodyParts3D/Anatomography** and **Z-Anatomy**.

The source datasets were systematically reviewed and modified to the best of
the author's anatomical knowledge. The work includes correction of existing
anatomy, reconstruction of structures with inaccurate morphology or position,
addition of structures absent from the source datasets, and improvement of
the organization and visualization of the anatomical models.

Particular emphasis was placed on the **central, peripheral, and autonomic
nervous systems**, including extensive development of cerebral white matter,
brainstem nuclei and pathways, cranial nerves, spinal pathways and nerves,
and visceral autonomic anatomy.

Additional modifications were made to the vascular, meningeal,
musculoskeletal, orbital, skull-base, and cardiac anatomy.

---

## About This Record

The list below contains **202 individual modifications and development steps**.

The entries are presented as a **chronological working record rather than a
consolidated list**.

Consequently, some anatomical structures appear more than once. This reflects
the actual development of the atlas: structures were frequently corrected,
expanded, or reconstructed in successive stages as increasingly detailed
anatomy was incorporated.

The modification record therefore documents not only the final anatomical
content but also the progressive development of the dataset.

## Scope and Interpretation

This modification record documents the development of **Anatomia Aperta 3D**
relative to the source anatomical datasets.

The terms **added**, **corrected**, **reconstructed**, **remodeled**, and
**refined** describe modifications made within the context of this project
and should not be interpreted as implying that the resulting models constitute
definitive anatomical representations.

Anatomical structures were developed to the best of the author's anatomical
knowledge and using established anatomical relationships as reference.

Anatomia Aperta 3D remains an evolving project. Further corrections,
refinements, and additions may therefore be incorporated into future releases.

Users who identify potential anatomical inaccuracies are encouraged to open
an issue in this repository, preferably accompanied by relevant anatomical
references.
