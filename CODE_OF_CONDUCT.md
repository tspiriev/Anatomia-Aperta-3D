# Code of Conduct

Anatomia Aperta 3D adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

In short: be respectful and constructive, focus on what is best for the project and its educational purpose, accept
feedback gracefully, and do not harass, insult or demean anyone. The full text of the Covenant applies.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at
**3Datlas@pm.me**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security
of the reporter will be respected. Maintainers follow the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
