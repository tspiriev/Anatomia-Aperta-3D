## 4. Anatomia Aperta 3D Contributions

The Anatomia Aperta 3D project includes extensive anatomical
modifications, corrections, reconstructions, and additions
to the original datasets.

These include:

- Reconstruction of central nervous system structures and pathways.
- Expansion of cranial and peripheral nerve anatomy.
- Reconstruction of spinal nerve roots and spinal pathways.
- Extensive development of autonomic and visceral nervous systems.
- Corrections to vascular and meningeal anatomy.
- Improvements to musculoskeletal and cardiac anatomy.
- Anatomical organization, materials, and 3D visualization.

A detailed record of 202 modifications is provided in:

MODIFICATIONS.md

The original contributions and eligible adaptations created
for Anatomia Aperta 3D are released under CC BY-SA 4.0.
