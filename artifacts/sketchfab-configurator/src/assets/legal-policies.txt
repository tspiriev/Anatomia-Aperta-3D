Anatomia Aperta 3D — Terms of Use

Anatomia Aperta 3D operates as a nonprofit educational initiative and may accept voluntary donations to support its development, maintenance, and continued availability. Donations are optional and do not affect access to the atlas or the licensing conditions applicable to its anatomical datasets.
By accessing or using this website, you acknowledge and agree to the following terms.
Educational and Research Purpose
The anatomical models, illustrations, annotations, descriptions, and other materials presented through Anatomia Aperta 3D are provided for educational, academic, and research purposes.
The atlas is not intended to provide medical advice and should not be considered a substitute for authoritative anatomical references, professional medical education, clinical judgment, diagnostic evaluation, or patient-specific medical assessment.
Anatomical Accuracy
The anatomical datasets incorporated into Anatomia Aperta 3D have undergone review, correction, reconstruction, reorganization, and expansion.
Every effort has been made to provide anatomically accurate representations, and modifications have been performed to the best of the author’s anatomical knowledge. Nevertheless, anatomical models are representations of human anatomy and may contain inaccuracies, simplifications, omissions, or technical limitations.
Users are responsible for independently verifying anatomical information when it is used for scientific, educational, clinical, or other professional purposes.
If an anatomical error is identified, users are encouraged to report it through the project’s GitHub repository, preferably with appropriate anatomical references.
Clinical Use
Anatomia Aperta 3D is not a medical device and has not been developed or validated for diagnosis, treatment, surgical navigation, patient-specific surgical planning, or other regulated clinical applications.
Any clinical or professional use of information obtained from the atlas remains the responsibility of the individual user.

Licensing & Attribution
Anatomia Aperta 3D — Licensing & Attribution
Anatomia Aperta 3D is committed to open anatomical education, transparent provenance, and appropriate attribution of the resources on which the project is built.
The atlas combines and develops anatomical material originating from several open resources. Consequently, users should consider the provenance and applicable license of the material they intend to reuse.
BodyParts3D / Anatomography
A substantial component of the anatomical geometry underlying Anatomia Aperta 3D originates from BodyParts3D/Anatomography, developed by the Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), Japan.
BodyParts3D has been distributed under Creative Commons licensing. The applicable license may depend upon the particular version of the source dataset.
Users should consult the original BodyParts3D documentation and the detailed licensing information provided in the Anatomia Aperta 3D GitHub repository before redistributing source-derived material.
Z-Anatomy
Anatomia Aperta 3D also builds upon Z-Anatomy — The Libre 3D Atlas of Anatomy.
Z-Anatomy itself incorporates material from multiple sources. Components derived from Z-Anatomy therefore remain subject to the applicable licensing and attribution requirements identified by the original project.
Anatomia Aperta 3D Modifications
The source anatomical resources have been extensively reviewed, corrected, reorganized, reconstructed, and expanded for Anatomia Aperta 3D.
This work includes anatomical corrections and additions, reconstruction of neural structures and pathways, organization of anatomical datasets, modification of materials, and development of the interactive 3D presentation and anatomical hierarchy.
A complete chronological record of these changes is publicly available in the project’s MODIFICATIONS.md document.
