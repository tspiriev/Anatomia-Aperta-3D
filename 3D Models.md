# Anatomia Aperta 3D — 3D Model Datasets

The complete 3D anatomical datasets of **Anatomia Aperta 3D** are
distributed separately from the GitHub repository because of their
large file sizes.

The models are provided in GLB format and are organized by anatomical
system.

## Available Datasets

### Skeletal System
Human skeletal system including individual bones and representations
of muscular attachment regions.

### Muscular System
Human muscular system including muscles of the head and face, neck,
back, thorax, abdomen, pelvis, upper limbs, and lower limbs, together
with associated fasciae and bursae.

### Joints and Ligaments
Human articulations and associated ligamentous structures of the axial
and appendicular skeleton.

### Cardiovascular System
Cardiac, arterial, and venous anatomy, including major regional
vascular structures.

### Nervous System
Extensive dataset of the central, peripheral, and autonomic nervous
systems, including brain and spinal cord anatomy, cranial and spinal
nerves, peripheral nerves, brachial and lumbar plexuses, brainstem nuclei, neural pathways,
white-matter systems, and autonomic and visceral nervous anatomy.

### Visceral Organs
Three-dimensional anatomy of the major thoracic, abdominal, and pelvic
organs and associated structures.

---

## File Format

The anatomical datasets are distributed primarily in **GLB (glTF
Binary)** format for compatibility with modern 3D visualization
software and web-based 3D viewers.
